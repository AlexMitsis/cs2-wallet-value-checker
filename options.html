BUFF → Steam wallet-value check

Read-only research aid. Prices come from Steam's undocumented public price endpoint (no login, no cookies), called only when you hover an item, cached and rate-limited. BUFF figures are the card's lowest listing price plus the fees you enter below — not the checkout total. Steam Wallet credit is not cash.

Where it works

  • Use Market → All Items: card price = cheapest sell listing (verified).
  • Best Deals / Popular: card shows one specific listing, maybe not the cheapest; the popup warns.
  • Recent Buy Orders, Sticker Combos, Gold Stickers: not supported (buy orders / sticker premiums); the popup won't compare.
  • Item detail pages and 2014 Katowice: no popup.
